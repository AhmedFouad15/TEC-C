<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Domain models with explicit units</name>
  <files>agent/tecc_brain/models.py, agent/tests/test_models.py</files>
  <action>
  Define Pydantic v2 models (use `model_config = ConfigDict(frozen=True, extra="forbid")` for static/market inputs; outputs may be mutable). Module docstring states the UNIT CONVENTION, and every field docstring/`Field(description=...)` repeats its unit:
  - Rates (APY, hurdle, PD, edges, scores) are **fractions per year** (0.0215 = 2.15%/yr). Convert to % only for display.
  - Money is **float USD**. On-chain USD×1e8 conversion happens only at the chain boundary (Phase 7) — put a helper `usd_to_onchain(usd: float) -> int` and `onchain_to_usd(v: int) -> float` here (×1e8, round half-even via `decimal`), because the contract measures radius in 8-decimal USD.
  - Probabilities in [0, 1].

  Enums: `RiskTier(IntEnum)` CONSERVATIVE=1, BALANCED=2, AGGRESSIVE=3 (matches TetherGuardHook constants). `ActionType(str, Enum)`: stake, deposit, optimize_yield, swap, withdraw, transfer, reduce_exposure, buy_discount. `AssetKind(str, Enum)`: native, stable_fiat, stable_crypto, stable_algorithmic, lst, gold, btc_wrapped. `RedemptionStatus(float-valued)`: use plain float field with allowed values {0.0, 0.5, 1.0} validated (open, delayed, paused). `AutonomyMode(str, Enum)`: AUTO, PROPOSE, BREAKOUT, BLOCKED. `CardLabel(str, Enum)`: GOOD, BETTER, BEST, REDUCE_EXPOSURE, OPPORTUNITY.

  Static data models:
  - `Asset`: symbol, name, kind, peg (Literal "USD","ETH","XAU" or None), tier (RiskTier), cluster (str|None), reserve_quality q∈[0,1], lgd∈[0,1], pd_base∈[0,1] (annual), redemption_fee∈[0,0.05], redemption_delay_days≥0, coverage_default∈[0,1.5] (claim per token honoured by redemption — see BRAIN_DESIGN §2.4).
  - `Venue` (a place money can go): id, name, protocol, action (ActionType), accepts (list of asset symbols), produces (asset symbol), tier, pd_base, lgd, entry_slippage_bps≥0, exit_slippage_bps≥0 (normal conditions), redemption_delay_days≥0, exposures (list of pegged asset symbols the position depends on, used by regime/contagion), target_address (str|None, filled in Phase 7).
  - `ContagionLink`: source, target, rho∈[0,1].

  Market input models:
  - `PegState`: symbol, price (in peg units, e.g. 0.88), sentiment_negative∈[0,1], redemption_status, liquidity_stress∈[0,1], coverage∈[0,1.5], source (str), as_of (datetime).
  - `VenueQuote`: venue_id, apy (fraction), depth_usd≥0, source, as_of.
  - `NewsSignal`: entity, p_negative∈[0,1], headline, source.
  - `MarketSnapshot`: as_of, hurdle_rate (1Y Shibor fraction), shibor (dict tenor→fraction), prices_usd (dict symbol→float), venues (dict id→VenueQuote), pegs (dict symbol→PegState), news (list[NewsSignal]), source (str, e.g. "snapshot:2026-10-16").

  Intent & decision models (contracts for later phases — keep them minimal but complete):
  - `Mandate`: raw_text, action, asset, amount (float, in asset units), amount_usd (float|None), horizon_days (int, default 365, >0), recipients (list[str], default []), constraints (list[str]), parser (Literal "rules","llm").
  - `ScoreBreakdown`: excess_yield, pd, lgd, expected_loss, loss_weighted, c_in, c_out_stress, affinity_term, total — all fractions/yr.
  - `Candidate`: venue_id, action, asset_in, amount_usd, apy, score (ScoreBreakdown|None), rejected_reasons (list[str]).
  - `Card`: label, candidate, mode (AutonomyMode), headline (str), explanation (list[str]).
  - `Decision`: mandate, mode, auto_amount_usd, proposed_amount_usd, cards (list[Card]), trace (list[str]).

  Avoid: business logic in models (no scoring here); `float` validators only for ranges. Do not use `Decimal` for rates — the math in Phase 2 is float and tests use tolerances.

  `tests/test_models.py`: (1) RiskTier values equal 1/2/3; (2) Asset rejects q=1.2 and extra fields; (3) PegState rejects redemption_status 0.3; (4) `usd_to_onchain(500.0) == 50_000_000_000` and round-trip; (5) MarketSnapshot round-trips through `model_dump_json`/`model_validate_json`.
