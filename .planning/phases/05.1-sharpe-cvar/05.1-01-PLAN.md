<objective>
Show Sharpe ratio and 95% CVaR for every scored option, computed from the same model the score uses.

Purpose: The team's architecture names an "Econometric & Liquidity agent (Sharpe, CVaR)". Judges know these metrics;
showing them next to the loss-averse score answers "why not just Sharpe?" (Sharpe treats upside as risk and misses
the impairment tail; CVaR sees the tail, and the score prices it with the user's λ).
Output: `tecc_brain/risk/metrics.py`, `vol_annual` per venue, ScoreBreakdown.sharpe/.cvar_95, card lines, tests.
</objective>
